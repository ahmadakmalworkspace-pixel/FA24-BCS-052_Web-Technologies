EP-133 K.O. II

Rs. 4500

Description

The EP-133 K.O. II is a sampler, sequencer and composer in one small box. You can record sounds around you with the built-in microphone, play them back and turn them into a full track without a computer.

A sampler records a short piece of sound, such as a drum hit, a voice or a single note, and lets you play it again whenever you like. A sequencer plays those sounds back in an order you choose, so a few sounds become a repeating beat or melody.

Once you have a beat, you can build it into a song by joining several patterns together. The built-in effects let you change the sound of one part or of the whole track, for example by adding echo or making it sound rougher.

Because it has its own speaker and runs on batteries or over USB-C, you can start making music anywhere. There is nothing to set up. Switch it on, record a sound and start playing.

It has 128MB of storage for your sounds and projects, so you can keep many ideas on the device and come back to them later.

Record a sound from the world around you, such as a door closing, a voice or a street noise. Trim it, play it at different pitches and place it in your beat. Small ideas like this often become the start of a whole track.

Keep adding layers one at a time. Start with a drum sound, add a bass sound, then add a melody on top. Listen after each step and remove anything that makes the track feel crowded.

When your track is ready, use the effects to give it a final character. A little echo can make a simple beat feel larger, and a rougher effect can make it feel more raw.
